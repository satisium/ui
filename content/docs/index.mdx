This is not just a component library; it is a spatial environment. We treat the browser window as a physical canvas. Every transition, every click, and every layout shift respects the laws of spring physics.

### Philosophy

Our design style is heavily inspired by modern design engineers. We obsess over:

- Spatial awareness and fluid states.
- Typography that scales flawlessly.
- Zero layout shift during animations.

Welcome to the future of frontend architecture.
