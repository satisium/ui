## Overview

Satisium UI is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

## Data We Collect

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the documentation experience and understand which components are most useful.

### Analytics Data

When you consent to analytics, we may collect:

- **Page views** — which documentation pages you visit
- **Events** — actions like copying code, using the command menu, or installing components
- **Performance metrics** — Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP) to monitor site speed
- **Errors** — client-side error messages (no stack traces or personal data)

### Public Metrics

We track aggregate, anonymous metrics for CLI installs and documentation views to display community interest. These metrics are not tied to individuals.

## Cookies

Analytics cookies are only set after you give explicit consent. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser data for this site.

## Third-Party Services

- **PostHog** — product analytics (self-hosted or cloud, depending on configuration)
- **Upstash Redis** — anonymous public counter storage
- **Cloudinary** — image and video delivery for component demos

## Data Retention

Analytics events are retained for 90 days, after which they are automatically deleted. Public metrics are retained indefinitely as aggregate counters.

## Your Rights

- **Opt out** — Use the consent banner to decline analytics
- **Access** — Contact us if you want to know what data is stored about you
- **Deletion** — Clear your browser data for this site to remove consent and associated identifiers

For privacy-related questions, open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/satisium/ui) or reach out via [X](https://x.com/iamsatish4564).
